- Process and fulfill your orders

## Information Sharing
We do NOT sell your personal information. We share data only with:

## Data Security
We use SSL encryption and secure servers. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure. We recommend using strong passwords and not sharing account credentials.

## Your Rights
You can:
- Access your data by logging into your account
- Request deletion of your data (email privacy@example.com)
- Opt out of marketing emails
- Request a copy of your data

## Cookies
We use cookies to:

You can disable cookies in your browser settings.
